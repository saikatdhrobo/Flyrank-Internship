### Key Design Decisions

1. **Three "Just Me" tasks** (instead of the minimum two) — studying research papers, practising algorithmic problem-solving, and reviewing peer PRs are all skills that atrophy if outsourced to AI. Each rationale explains why delegation would defeat the learning purpose.

2. **Tasks are concrete and specific** — each entry references real tools (FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Alembic, pytest, Docker Compose), real frequencies, and a real context (study-group project, internship applications, mentor updates).

3. **Classification spread is realistic** — the mix (3 Just Me, 4 Delegate with Review, 3 Collaborate, 2 Fully Automate) reflects a student who is thoughtful about AI use rather than either resisting or over-delegating.

4. **Target tasks are measurable** — each "Done Well" criterion is binary-checkable (passes tests, builds without warnings, under 15 minutes of edits, etc.), making success unambiguous.

5. **Placeholder is clearly flagged** — the README and the placeholder filename both instruct the user to replace it with a real screenshot.
